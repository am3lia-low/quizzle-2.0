## Outcomes

### 🏖️ Beach Holiday
*Sun on your face, sand in your bag, zero plans.*

You know that rest is a skill, and you're very good at it. Give you warm sand, a good book and a cold drink, and you'll recharge like nobody else. Friends envy how easily you switch off, and how relaxed you look when you come back.

Traits: laid-back, sunny, easygoing

### 🏙️ City Break
*Three days, twelve galleries, one very good pair of shoes.*

You love a place with a pulse. Museums in the morning, a hidden bar at night and a walk through every neighbourhood in between. You plan well, you notice details, and you come home with a list of places everyone else wants to borrow.

Traits: curious, stylish, organised

### 🥾 Mountain Trek
*If there's a peak, you're already lacing your boots.*

You like a holiday you can feel in your legs. Early starts, big views and the quiet pride of getting somewhere the hard way. You're steady, determined and great company on a long day, especially when you share the good snacks at the top.

Traits: determined, outdoorsy, resilient

### 🚐 Road Trip
*The destination is optional. The playlist is not.*

For you the fun is in the going. Detours, roadside diners and towns you'd never heard of until you were in them. You're spontaneous, flexible and the best storyteller in any group, mostly because things keep happening to you.

Traits: spontaneous, free-spirited, fun

### 🌲 Cabin Retreat
*A fire, a blanket and absolutely no signal.*

Your ideal getaway is somewhere quiet enough to hear yourself think. Long walks, slow breakfasts and evenings by the stove with the people you love most. You're thoughtful and warm, and you make any place feel like home within an hour.

Traits: cosy, thoughtful, grounded

### 🍜 Food Tour
*You plan trips around dinner, and lunch, and snacks.*

You get to know a place through its kitchens. Night markets, family-run bakeries and the stall with the longest queue. You're open-minded, generous with recommendations and the person everyone texts when they need somewhere to eat.

Traits: adventurous, generous, open-minded

## Agent notes

## Design choices

- **9 questions, 6 outcomes, winner scoring.** Holidays split cleanly into distinct types (beach, city break, mountain trek, road trip, cabin retreat, food tour), so `winner` fits better than `blend`. Six outcomes covers the main kinds of getaway without results feeling interchangeable; nine questions gives each outcome enough chances to score.
- Mostly single-outcome +2 answers, with a few split answers (Saturday morning coffee, itinerary question) to add texture.
- **Theme:** a vintage travel kit. Cream paper, ink-navy text and teal accent; airmail red/teal stripes across the top of the page and around the intro "postcard"; answers styled as luggage tags with a punched hole that fills when picked; the result card gets a tilted passport-style ✈ stamp in the outcome colour. Abril Fatface + Work Sans.

## Critic notes

The critic ran three times.

- **Round 1 (5 blocking):** "Whatever the local radio plays" wasn't a music style; "Head out solo and see what happens" overlapped the road-trip answer and fit City Break poorly; "A duffel bag and a cooler of snacks" was two ideas; "A muggy summer evening" bundled weather + season + time; Food Tour scored at both ends of the itinerary question. Fixed all five (new answers, itinerary scoring rebalanced so Road Trip leads the "loose list" answer and City Break alone owns "every hour accounted for").
- **Round 2 (2 blocking):** "Breezy bossa nova" → Food Tour didn't make sense and overlapped reggae; three near-identical hot weathers in Q5. Replaced the soundtrack question with "Pick a sound to fall asleep to" (waves, pines, traffic, rain on a tent, tyres, night market) and cut Q5 to five distinct weathers (Food Tour no longer appears there; it got a 2-point answer in Q1 instead).
- **Round 3:** no blocking issues. Acted on two notes: swapped the odd "Pans clattering downstairs" for "A night market winding down", and the repeated knitwear motif ("A hand-knitted blanket" → "A pine-scented candle").
- **Left as is:** Q1 (Saturday morning) and Q3 (cancelled plans) both touch on free time; they're a preference vs a scenario and the critic rated it non-blocking. The outcome id `adventure` for "Mountain Trek" is internal only. "Highway" kept alongside UK spelling elsewhere.

## Uncertainties

- Food Tour is the rarest result (~11.5%). Intended to be fine, but check it doesn't feel too hard to get.
- Weakest scoring links: "Mild and overcast" → City Break, "A half-empty suitcase" → Food Tour (room to bring food home), "Sunshine and showers" → Road Trip.
- Ties before tie-break are ~23.5% because most answers give a flat +2.
- This quiz took the plain `2026-W48` ID because no quiz existed for the week yet when this ran (early Monday SGT). If the regular Monday Routine runs after this PR opens, it will see W48 as taken and stop.
