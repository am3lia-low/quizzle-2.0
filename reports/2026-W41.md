## Outcomes

### 🫖 Masala Chai
*Bold, spiced and the heart of every gathering.*

You bring warmth, a little kick and a lot of stories. Friends gravitate to your kitchen because something is always simmering and the conversation is always good. You are generous with your time and unafraid of strong flavours.

Traits: spirited, generous, lively

### 🌼 Jasmine Green
*Fresh, graceful and quietly uplifting.*

You have a gentle way of making a day feel lighter. You notice small beauties, keep your calm and leave a lovely impression wherever you go. People feel more like themselves around you.

Traits: graceful, fresh, gentle

### 🎩 Earl Grey
*Classic, polished and effortlessly put together.*

You like things done properly, with a twist of something unexpected. Reliable, well read and good with a dry joke, you are the person everyone trusts to know the right thing to say.

Traits: refined, dependable, witty

### 🌙 Chamomile
*Soft, soothing and exactly what everyone needs.*

You are the exhale at the end of a long day. You listen well, you never rush anyone and your home has the best blankets. Your calm is a gift that others lean on more than you know.

Traits: soothing, patient, caring

### 🧋 Bubble Tea
*Playful, colourful and always up for an adventure.*

You turn errands into outings and outings into stories. Curious, sociable and full of ideas, you always have a new favourite thing to share, and extra toppings are non-negotiable.

Traits: playful, adventurous, sociable
