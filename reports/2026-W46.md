## Outcomes

### ⛈️ Storm Caller
*Big gestures, bright sparks and a cape that's always flapping.*

Your magic is loud, bold and best watched from a safe distance. You leap first and work out the landing on the way down, and somehow it usually works. People feel braver standing next to you, and nobody ever forgets the day you turned up.

Traits: bold, spontaneous, electric

### 🌿 Hedge Wizard
*Herbs on the windowsill, soup on the stove, a cure for everything.*

Your magic is the everyday kind that actually helps: a balm for a scraped knee, a tea for a bad night, a garden that feeds the whole lane. You're steady and practical, and your kitchen is where everyone ends up when they need looking after.

Traits: grounded, nurturing, resourceful

### 🔭 Stargazer
*Up the tallest tower, reading tomorrow in the sky.*

You see patterns other people miss and you're happiest with a quiet night and a big question. Friends come to you for perspective, because you always seem to be looking a little further ahead. Your notebooks are full of dreams, maps and very good hunches.

Traits: intuitive, reflective, far-sighted

### ✨ Enchanter
*One smile, one sparkle, and the whole room is on your side.*

Your magic works on people, and you use it for good: soothing arguments, starting parties and making strangers feel like old friends. You know exactly the right word at the right moment. Half your spells are just charm, and honestly that's the best half.

Traits: charming, sociable, persuasive

### ⚗️ Alchemist
*Scorch marks on the ceiling, a breakthrough in the notebook.*

You want to know how everything works, and then you want to make it work better. You're patient with experiments and impatient with 'because that's how it's done'. Your workshop is a glorious mess of bubbling jars, and every failure is just data.

Traits: curious, methodical, inventive

### 🦉 Beast Keeper
*Owls on your shoulder, foxes at your door, friends with everything.*

Your magic is mostly listening, and every creature in the forest seems to know it. You're gentle, observant and fiercely loyal to the ones you love, whether they have two legs, four or wings. Wherever you settle, something small and furry moves in.

Traits: gentle, loyal, observant

## Agent notes

## Design choices
- **9 questions, 6 outcomes.** Six kinds of wizard (Storm Caller, Hedge Wizard, Stargazer, Enchanter, Alchemist, Beast Keeper) each match a clear temperament: bold, nurturing, reflective, sociable, curious, gentle. Nine questions give each one a fair chance without the quiz dragging.
- **Winner scoring.** The six are distinct types, not overlapping styles, so a single result fits better than a blend.
- **Questions** mix library, home, fair and chest "pick" questions with scenarios (midnight knock, spell gone wrong, helping a friend ask someone out) and a short three-way pick ("Trying something new, you…").
- **Theme:** a wizard's night sky. Deep indigo with scattered CSS-gradient stars and a soft violet glow, gold accents, Cinzel headings, answers styled as gilded spell cards, and a gently floating emoji (turned off under reduced motion).

## Critic notes
- **First pass, blocking:**
  - Q7 "Fast and loud / Slow and careful" combined two ideas per answer and favoured Storm Caller. It became "Dive straight in / Read the instructions first / Watch someone else try first", with points spread evenly.
  - "A greenhouse with a wood stove" had two ideas. It became "A greenhouse inside the kitchen".
  - "A compass that points to adventure" was a weak, too-appealing link. It became "A cloak that catches the wind".
- **First pass, non-blocking:**
  - Intro mentioned towers that never appear. Changed to libraries.
  - "Someone small" read as a child. Changed to "A pet".
  - "A mirror that flatters everyone" read as vain. Changed to "A ring that makes strangers smile".
  - Added a Beast Keeper answer to Q4.
- **Second pass, blocking:**
  - "Peek through the keyhole first" was a weak Alchemist link and overlapped the Stargazer answer. It became "Ask a trick question through the door".
  - Q3 balcony and porch aren't rooms. The question now asks for an unusual "feature".
  - "Bake something irresistible for the date" didn't help with asking someone out. It became "Make them a calming cup of tea".
- **Left as is:**
  - "A brass telescope" (Stargazer) and "A workshop with a scorched ceiling" (Alchemist) are fairly direct pointers, but they make sense and don't name the outcome.
  - "Accounts of famous shipwrecks" for Storm Caller is a little dark, but it's a library shelf.

## Uncertainties
- Q8's chest mixes ordinary items (telescope, seeds, bottles) with magical ones (cloak, ring, whistle). Worth a quick read to see if it feels uneven.
- "Reading fortunes in a little tent" (Q5) is a generic fairground stall. The critic flagged it as a mild trope; I think it's fine, but have a look.
- The fix attempt count is 1 because the first check passed. The two later re-runs followed critic edits and both passed.
