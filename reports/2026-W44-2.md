## Outcomes

### 🐙 Octopus
*Eight arms, three hearts, and a plan for everything.*

You're the clever one who can't leave a puzzle alone. Locked jar? Tricky problem? You'll find a way in, or out. Real octopuses are famous escape artists and quick learners, and you share their gift for improvising with whatever's to hand. Life is never boring around you.

Traits: curious, inventive, resourceful

### 🐢 Sea Turtle
*Calm, unhurried, and always finds the way home.*

You play the long game and you don't rattle easily. Sea turtles cross whole oceans and still find their way back to the beach they hatched on, and you have that same quiet sense of direction. People trust your perspective because you've clearly thought things through.

Traits: patient, steady, wise

### 🐬 Dolphin
*The life of the pod and everyone's favourite plus-one.*

You bring the energy. Dolphins live in close-knit groups, play just for fun and even use signature whistles a bit like names, and you're the friend who knows everyone's name at the party. You make plans happen and people happier.

Traits: playful, sociable, upbeat

### 🦦 Sea Otter
*Cosy, loyal, and holding on to the people who matter.*

Sea otters hold paws while they sleep so they don't drift apart, and that's very you. You keep your people close, remember the little things and turn any place into somewhere comfortable. Being around you feels like coming home.

Traits: loyal, caring, cosy

### 🐋 Humpback Whale
*A big heart and a song that carries for miles.*

You feel things deeply and you're not afraid to show it. Humpback whales sing long, complex songs that change over time, and you're the one with the playlist for every mood and the friend who asks how someone is really doing. Gentle, thoughtful and quietly huge in people's lives.

Traits: empathetic, deep, gentle

### 🐡 Pufferfish
*Small, sweet, and surprisingly mighty when it counts.*

You take a while to warm up to people, but once you do, you'll defend them fiercely. Behind the careful exterior is a secret artist: one small pufferfish spends days sculpting intricate circles in the sand. You're a true original with your own very specific taste.

Traits: original, honest, protective

## Agent notes

## Design choices

- **9 questions, 6 outcomes:** Octopus, Sea Turtle, Dolphin, Sea Otter, Humpback Whale, Pufferfish. Six well-known animals give a clear spread of personalities (clever, patient, social, cosy, big-hearted, small-but-mighty). Nine questions are enough to separate six results.
- **Winner scoring:** each creature is a distinct type, not a blend.
- Each description includes one real animal fact: octopus escape artists, turtles homing to their natal beach, dolphin signature whistles, otters holding paws, humpback songs, and the white-spotted pufferfish's sand circles.
- **Theme:** "sunlight from the surface". There's a teal glow at the top fading to deep navy, with faint bubbles. The fonts are rounded (Baloo 2 / Nunito), and the result card is outlined in each creature's colour. The emoji bobs gently, and this is disabled under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **Check attempts:** the first check run failed in playtest because a hover `translateY` on answers made the buttons jitter under the cursor. I removed it and every run after that passed.

## Critic notes

The critic ran three times.

**Pass 1** flagged 6 blocking question-logic issues:
- Q1 answers with bolted-on ideas ("The park, with whoever's free").
- Q4 answers that were the same size ("A few close friends" / "A small group of new faces").
- Q5 problems: "A puzzle or a clever gadget" was two items, "A surprise party" isn't a gift, and two answers overlapped.
- Q6: the ambiguous "Find a clever way to leave".
- Q8: the packing question mixed timing, amount and contents.
- Q9: answers in mismatched shapes, plus an overlap between "Perspective" and "An honest opinion".

All of these were rewritten. Q8 became "Pick a travel bag." and Q4 now offers pure group sizes.

**Pass 2** flagged three new problems:
- "Eight of us" → Octopus was too on the nose.
- "A hard-shell suitcase" → Pufferfish was the wrong animal (shell = turtle). I swapped it with the carry-on.
- "A puzzle box" and "A board game" overlapped, so the board game became "A karaoke machine".

**Pass 3** flagged that a big group → Sea Turtle didn't make sense. Now "Just me" gives Sea Turtle and Pufferfish a point each, and "Five or six of us" gives Octopus and Dolphin a point each. That also closed a gap in the sizes.

Smaller changes taken from the notes:
- The quiz emoji was 🐙, the same as the Octopus result, so it is now 🌊.
- "Teleporting" → "Super speed" for Dolphin.
- "An open-air concert" → "A rooftop at sunset", to cut a repeated music link for Whale.

Left as is (non-blocking):
- "Invisibility" → Pufferfish is a slight stretch.
- "A huge duffel bag" → Sea Otter and "A well-worn canvas tote" → Whale are soft links.
- "A hard-shell suitcase" → Sea Turtle is a gentle wink.

## Uncertainties

- Q8 ("Pick a travel bag.") has the weakest links of the quiz. It's worth playing to see if it feels arbitrary.
- Two animal facts are simplified. Mainly female turtles return, often to the region rather than the exact beach. Otters "hold paws" but more often wrap in kelp.
- About 24% of random playthroughs tie before the tie-break, so the last question settles a fair share of results. Dolphin is slightly ahead at about 19.5%.
