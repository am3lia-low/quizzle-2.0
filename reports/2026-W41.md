## Outcomes

### 🎩 Earl Grey
*Classic, quick-witted and quietly a little bit fancy.*

You have excellent taste and you know it, but you wear it lightly. You notice the detail everyone else missed, you love a good story with some history behind it, and your dry one-liners land every time. You're proof that the classics are classics for a reason.

### 🌼 Chamomile
*Gentle, unhurried and the calmest person in any room.*

You have a gift for slowing things down. People feel at ease around you without quite knowing why. You'd pick a quiet corner over a crowd, a soft blanket over a big night out, and you're the one everyone texts when they need someone to just listen.

### 🫚 Masala Chai

### 🧋 Bubble Tea

### 🔥 Lapsang Souchong

You're an acquired taste and proud of it. You chase adventures other people only daydream about, you'd rather have a wild story than a safe evening, and the people who get you really get you. Nobody forgets meeting you.

Traits: bold, adventurous, independent

## Agent notes

## Design choices

- **9 questions, 6 outcomes, winner scoring.** The six teas (Earl Grey, Chamomile, Masala Chai, Sencha, Bubble Tea, Lapsang Souchong) are distinct personalities rather than overlapping styles, so most points wins. Nine questions gives every outcome a point source in nearly every question while keeping the quiz quick.
- Questions mix scenarios (cancelled plans, group-project snag), preferences (weather, superpower, hobby, sleep sound, party spot) and one strict either/or (planned route vs wandering). No answer mentions tea.
- **Theme:** a tea-house look. Warm paper background with a faint scatter of tea-leaf shapes, Cormorant Garamond headings, steam that rises from the intro teapot (switched off under reduced motion), a progress bar that "pours" from gold to chai-brown, and result cards edged like a saucer in the outcome colour.

## Critic notes

This is a re-run of W41 after the previous PR was closed for answers that didn't fit their questions, so both critic passes were told to be strict about the question-logic rules.

- **Pass 1 (blocking):** "A home-cooked meal" doesn't work with "You send…"; "Total silence" isn't a sound; "Stay calm and listen to everyone" was two ideas and echoed Chamomile's "calm" trait; "The bookshelf" wasn't a place like the other party spots; "Run a bath and light a candle" was two ideas. Fixed: "A huge care package", "Whirring fans", "Listen to every side", "The study", "Run a long hot bath".
- **Pass 1 (non-blocking, acted on):** Masala Chai leaned too hard on food, so "Cooking" became "Choir singing". "Mind reading" was a weak link for Chai and was replaced. Lapsang had two fire answers, so "A crackling fire" was dropped. "Calligraphy" moved to "Woodworking" so Sencha didn't lean on a cultural cliché. Chamomile now gets a full 2 points for "A handwritten card", and Earl Grey gets its own birthday answer. Earl Grey's emoji no longer duplicates the quiz's 🫖.
- **Pass 2 (blocking):** "Being in two places at once" didn't match the other superpowers in shape and overlapped with "Teleportation", so it became "Cloning". "A vintage postcard" and "A handwritten card" were interchangeable, so the postcard became "A first-edition book". Both fixed.
- **Pass 2 (non-blocking):** Lapsang had repeated travel imagery, so "Distant trains" became "Howling wind". Earl Grey's 🍋 emoji became 🎩, since its flavour is bergamot, not lemon. Its description's tautology was rewritten.
- **Left as is:** "Pottery" and "Woodworking" are both crafts, but they're distinct enough. "Crisp frost" and "Snowfall" are both cold weathers, but they feel different. Sencha gets no points in the house-party question. Chamomile is technically a herbal infusion, which is common usage. Ties before tie-break are about 18%, handled by the engine.
