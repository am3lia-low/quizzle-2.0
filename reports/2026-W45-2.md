## Outcomes

### 🔮 The Oracle
*Calm, far-sighted and usually right in the end.*

You see the big picture before anyone else has finished reading the question. You think deeply, you'd rather be wise than quick, and when you finally weigh in, people listen. Your best ideas arrive in the shower.

Traits: wise, patient, intuitive

### 📊 The Analyst
*Finds the pattern, checks the facts, shows the working.*

Your mind is a beautifully organised filing cabinet. You love evidence, clear reasoning and a well-made chart, and you're the person who quietly spots the mistake everyone else missed. Precise, fair and brilliant.

Traits: logical, precise, fair-minded

### 🎨 The Dreamweaver
*Turns a blank page into a whole new world.*

Give you a prompt and you'll come back with ten ideas, three of them wonderful and one of them gloriously weird. You think in pictures, stories and what-ifs, and you make everyone around you a little more imaginative.

Traits: imaginative, playful, original

### 🔧 The Tinkerer
*Learns by building, breaking and building again.*

Instructions are optional; experiments are not. You understand things by getting your hands on them, and there's nothing you won't try to fix with a bit of patience and a spare part. Practical, inventive and hard to stump.

Traits: hands-on, inventive, resourceful

### 💬 The Companion
*Reads the room and makes everyone feel heard.*

Your superpower is people. You remember the little details, you know when someone needs a joke or a quiet moment, and conversations with you leave people feeling better than before. Smart, yes, but kind first.

Traits: empathetic, warm, attentive

### 🛰️ The Explorer
*Always one tab away from a brand-new obsession.*

Your curiosity has no off switch. You follow every interesting thread to see where it goes, you collect fun facts like souvenirs and you're never happier than when you're somewhere (or learning something) completely new.

Traits: curious, adventurous, open-minded

## Agent notes

## Design choices
- **Topic:** requested directly by the owner ("what kind of AI matches your mind"). It wasn't in `topics.json`, so this PR leaves `topics.json` untouched.
- **7 questions, 6 outcomes:** six friendly AI "personalities" built around thinking styles: The Oracle (big-picture intuition), The Analyst (logic and evidence), The Dreamweaver (generative imagination), The Tinkerer (hands-on building), The Companion (empathy) and The Explorer (curiosity). They're archetypes, not real products or brands. Seven questions keeps it snappy.
- **Blend scoring:** thinking styles overlap a lot, so showing the mix (e.g. 50% Analyst, 30% Tinkerer) is more fun and more honest than one label.
- **Theme:** a friendly, softly glowing circuit grid on deep navy, with mint and lavender glows, Space Grotesk headings, a blinking terminal cursor after the title (off under reduced motion), glowing answer buttons and result cards that glow in each outcome's colour.

## Critic notes
Four critic rounds.
- **Round 1:** "Shake it, flip it and try everything" bundled several ideas and overlapped Tinkerer. The rainy-afternoon game picks overlapped each other (Pictionary/Charades, Riddles/Sudoku/Rubik's cube). Fixed both: replaced the Q1 answer and swapped in a different question.
- **Round 2:** "Pick a superpower." was reused from earlier quizzes. The puzzle-box and can't-answer questions tested the same thing. "A parrot" → Dreamweaver read as a dig ("stochastic parrot"). Fixed all of these.
- **Round 3:** three questions were word-for-word or near reuses of earlier quizzes ("Your friends are planning a trip…", "Pick a pet.", "Your friends come to you for…"). The museum question had non-museum answers. Replaced all of them with new questions, including a treehouse scenario, a voucher pick, an adventure kit and a book pick. I checked every new question text against all existing quizzes.
- **Round 4:** "A dinner out with friends" didn't fit "spend on yourself", and "A notebook" vs "A sketchbook" were too alike. Fixed both: "A stack of greeting cards" and "A magnifying glass". Also changed the Oracle answers to "Picturing the finished result" and "A star chart", and dropped the repeated train motif for Explorer.
- **Left as is:** four of the seven questions are "pick a thing" questions. The outcomes read as human thinking styles with light AI framing (intro, "Boot me up", "Your AI match is"). That felt warmer than robot-themed results.

## Uncertainties
- The checks were run 6 times, after each round of edits. Every run passed; none failed.
- Ties before tie-break are 30%, higher than usual because most answers give 2 points to a single outcome. Blend scoring shows the mix anyway.
- Answers are listed in the same outcome order in each question. Check whether the engine shuffles them; if not, a sharp player may spot the pattern.
