## Outcomes

### 🌿 Monstera
*Big leaves, big heart, takes up the whole corner and nobody minds.*

You fill a room in the best way. You host the dinners, you have the big ideas and you always make space for one more. Give you a bit of room and you'll grow into something everyone notices, splitting new leaves just when people thought you couldn't get any more impressive.

Traits: generous, bold, expansive

### 🌱 Snake Plant
*Unbothered, upright and quietly coping in any light.*

Forgotten for three weeks? Still standing. You're the steady one: calm in a crisis, low on drama and reliable in a way that makes everyone feel safe. You don't need much fuss to do well, and your easy, unshakeable calm rubs off on the people around you.

Traits: resilient, steady, dependable

### 🍃 Pothos
*Gets along with everyone and somehow ends up on every shelf.*

You're easy company and you fit in anywhere: a new job, a new city, a friend's sofa. You trail happily into every corner of people's lives, and give a bit of yourself to anyone who asks; half your friends have a cutting of you growing in a jar of water.

Traits: adaptable, friendly, easygoing

### 🌳 Fiddle-Leaf Fig
*Particular about the details, and stunning once it's just right.*

You know exactly how you like things, and that's not a flaw, it's taste. The right light, the right spot, the right plan. Move you around too much and you'll let people know, but once you're settled you're the most striking thing in the room.

Traits: discerning, stylish, precise

### 🌵 Succulent
*Sun-seeking, self-sufficient and happiest with a bit of space.*

You carry what you need and head off where the light is. Independent and sunny, you're the friend who books the solo trip and comes back glowing. You don't need much looking after, and you're surprisingly good at looking after yourself.

Traits: independent, sunny, adventurous

### 🌙 Prayer Plant
*Opens up by day, folds in for a cosy night.*

You live by rhythms: the morning routine, the evening wind-down, the Sunday reset. You're thoughtful and in tune with how you feel, and you know when to open up and when to fold in and recharge. People find your calm, steady patterns quietly reassuring.

Traits: thoughtful, intuitive, grounded

## Agent notes

## Design choices
- **8 questions, 6 outcomes:** Six popular houseplants with clearly different "personalities" (Monstera, Snake Plant, Pothos, Fiddle-Leaf Fig, Succulent, Prayer Plant), each based on a real habit of the plant: monstera's big split leaves, the snake plant's tolerance of neglect, trailing pothos that roots from cuttings, the fussy fiddle-leaf fig, sun-loving succulents, and prayer-plant leaves that fold up at night. Eight questions give each outcome about 7 answer slots.
- **Winner scoring:** these are distinct types rather than overlapping styles, so a single result reads better than a blend.
- **Theme, "Greenhouse":** sage background with faint glasshouse pane lines and a warm sunlight glow, a Young Serif / DM Sans font pairing, terracotta-pot primary buttons, leaf-shaped answer buttons with a 🌱 that turns into 🌿 when picked, and a result card styled like a plant-shop label tag in the outcome's colour. A gentle sway on the intro emoji, turned off under reduced motion.

## Critic notes
- **Round 1, blocking:**
  - Q1 (friend cancels), Q5 (Pick a superpower) and Q6 (friends planning a trip, your job) reused questions from W41/W42. Replaced them with "new flat", "Pick a way to travel." and "dinner-party guest".
  - Q1 had two overlapping answers and a steering clause. Q3's "plus extras to share" steered the answer. Too many questions and answers were about trips, which all went to Succulent.
  - All of these were fixed, and the Q8 trip answer was replaced too.
  - The Snake Plant tagline said "thriving in any light", which overstates it, and the description hinted at the debunked air-purifying claim. Both were softened.
- **Round 2, blocking:**
  - Q4 "Which room in a house is most you?" was too close to W41's "Which part of a house party is yours?". Replaced it with "Pick a view from your window."
  - "Heads home early, right on schedule" had a steering add-on. Trimmed it to "Heads home early".
- **Round 2, non-blocking, taken:**
  - "Reorganising my bookshelf" duplicated Q1's shelf-arranging answer, so it's now "Perfecting a new recipe".
  - Trimmed "Make up the bed for a proper night's sleep" and "One small bag, sorted in ten minutes".
- **Left as is:**
  - Some outcomes still have a recognisable motif (Succulent gets the sunny answers, Prayer Plant the restful ones). That's part of the plant link and not against any rule.
  - The links for Q2 "Spring" → Pothos and "Winter" → Snake Plant are a little loose.
  - The last round's text-only fixes weren't re-reviewed by the critic.
