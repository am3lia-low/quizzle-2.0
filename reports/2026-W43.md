## Outcomes

### 🎤 Lead Singer
*Front and centre, and somehow it never looks like effort.*

You light up a room without trying and you're not shy about owning the moment. People want to be around your energy, and you're brilliant at making everyone else feel like part of the show. Someone has to say the bold thing first, and it's usually you.

Traits: charismatic, expressive, bold

### 🎸 Lead Guitarist
*The flourish everyone remembers on the way home.*

You've got flair, skill and a healthy love of a big moment. You put real hours into the things you care about, and when you finally show them off, it lands. You're the friend with the dramatic stories and the even more dramatic sunglasses.

Traits: passionate, skilled, dramatic

### 🎵 Bassist
*Quietly holding everything together, and loving every minute.*

You're the calm, steady core of every group you're in. You don't need the spotlight to know you matter, and the people who get it really get it. You're relaxed, loyal and a lot cooler than you let on.

Traits: steady, loyal, low-key cool

### 🥁 Drummer
*Pure energy, perfect timing and the loudest laugh in the room.*

You keep everyone moving. You're fun, a little chaotic and surprisingly reliable when it counts: the plan only happens because you set the pace. Life's better at full volume, and your friends know you'll always turn up.

Traits: energetic, fun-loving, dependable

### 🎹 Keyboardist
*The secret genius behind the sound.*

You're thoughtful, inventive and full of ideas other people didn't know they needed. You love tinkering until something is just right, and you notice details everyone else misses. Quiet in a crowd, brilliant one-to-one.

Traits: inventive, thoughtful, detail-loving

## Agent notes

## Design choices
- **5 outcomes:** Lead Singer, Lead Guitarist, Bassist, Drummer and Keyboardist. That's the classic line-up, and each role maps to a clear temperament: spotlight, flair, steady core, energy, inventive detail.
- **8 questions:** enough to separate five outcomes without dragging. The questions mix scenarios (a queue, giving feedback), quick picks (pet, sport, jacket, job) and a lifestyle pick (Friday night).
- **Winner scoring:** band roles are distinct types, not a blend.
- **Answers avoid music:** none of them mention instruments or singing, so nobody can game their way to the role they want.
- **Theme, "gig night":** a dark stage lit by pink and teal spotlights, with the Bungee display font. Answers are styled as a numbered setlist with a taped edge, and the picked answer glows. The result card is a backstage pass in the outcome's colour, with a lanyard slot. The intro emoji strums gently, and the animation is off under reduced motion.

## Critic notes
There were three critic rounds.
- **Round 1, 6 blocking issues:**
  - "Pick a superpower." was reused verbatim from W41.
  - Two-idea answers: "Keeping the spreadsheet and the passports", "Tell them it's solid and offer to help" and "Karaoke, and I'm going first".
  - "A sleeveless one" was not parallel with the other jacket answers.
  - Overlapping wedding-mic answers.
  - Fixes: I replaced the superpower, group-trip and wedding questions. While checking for reuse, I found the party-arrival and group-trip questions were near-copies of earlier quizzes, so I replaced those too. I rewrote the other answers.
- **Round 2, 5 blocking issues:**
  - The birthday and Friday-night questions tested the same thing.
  - "Hype it up first, then add one note" was two ideas.
  - The jacket list was not parallel ("Sequins"), and the link from "Nylon" to Drummer didn't make sense.
  - "Lighthouse keeper" contradicted the Bassist outcome.
  - Fixes: I replaced birthday with a blank-wall question, jacket with sport, and reworked the dream jobs.
- **Round 3:** no blocking issues. I acted on one note and swapped "A giant calendar of plans" (not a decoration) for "A huge neon sign".
- **Left as is:**
  - Lead Guitarist has no answer in the queue question.
  - "Cheer them on loudly" is a soft kind of feedback, but people really do respond that way.
  - Peacock and octopus are whimsical pets.
