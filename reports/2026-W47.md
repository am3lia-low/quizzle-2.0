## Outcomes

### 🌑 New Moon
*Quiet, private and secretly full of plans.*

You do your best thinking where nobody's watching. People rarely see your ideas until they're ready, and then they're surprised how much was happening under the surface. You like fresh starts, clean slates and a little bit of mystery.

Traits: private, intentional, full of potential

### 🌒 Waxing Crescent
*The hopeful first sliver of something brand new.*

You're the friend who says "what if we tried…" and means it. New hobbies, new places, new people: you love the feeling of something just beginning. Your optimism is contagious, and it gets a lot of good things off the ground.

Traits: hopeful, curious, eager

### 🌓 First Quarter
*Half lit, fully committed. Decisions are your thing.*

When things get stuck, you're the one who makes the call. You'd rather choose and adjust than wait around for the perfect moment. Obstacles feel like a challenge rather than a stop sign, and people trust you to keep things moving.

Traits: bold, decisive, determined

### 🌔 Waxing Gibbous
*Almost there, and making sure it's perfect.*

You love the bit just before the big finish, when you can polish, tweak and refine. Details matter to you, and you notice the ones everyone else misses. Whatever you're working on, it's better for having passed through your hands.

Traits: meticulous, patient, devoted

### 🌕 Full Moon
*Bright, bold and impossible to miss.*

You light up a room without even trying. You feel things fully, say what you think and love a big night with your favourite people. Everyone looks up when you arrive, and you make sure they have a great time once you do.

Traits: radiant, expressive, magnetic

### 🌖 Waning Gibbous
*Generous with everything you've learned.*

You're the one people come to for advice, recipes, recommendations and a second opinion. You love passing on what you know, and you do it warmly. Your kitchen, your bookshelf and your time are all open to the people you care about.

Traits: generous, wise, nurturing

### 🌗 Last Quarter
*Honest, clear-headed and great at letting go.*

You know when something has run its course, and you're not afraid to say so. Clearing out, simplifying and moving on feel freeing to you, not sad. Friends love your honesty because it always comes with kindness and a clear way forward.

Traits: honest, clear-headed, unburdened

### 🌘 Waning Crescent
*Soft, dreamy and expert at a proper rest.*

You know that rest is part of the cycle, not a break from it. Cosy evenings, long baths and early nights are your idea of a treat. You're gentle with yourself and others, and you come back from every pause with fresh ideas.

Traits: restful, reflective, gentle

## Agent notes

## Design choices

- **8 outcomes** (preset by topics.json): one per lunar phase, from New Moon to Waning Crescent. Each phase is read as a stage of a cycle: beginning, hoping, deciding, refining, shining, sharing, letting go, resting.
- **10 questions:** with 8 outcomes, each one needs enough 2-point answers to be reachable without anyone feeling left out. Most questions have 6 answers. The mix is scenarios (free evening, stuck group project, criticism, party), "pick a…" choices (notebook, sound, hour, gift) and taste (wardrobe, TV).
- **Winner scoring:** the phases are distinct types, and a blend of "62% Full Moon / 38% New Moon" would read oddly.
- **"Pick an hour"** quietly follows real astronomy. The waxing crescent is up at 6 pm, the first quarter is high at 9 pm, the full moon peaks at midnight, the waning crescent rises around 3 am, the last quarter is high at 6 am, and the new moon is up with the sun at noon.
- **Theme:** a pale silver "lunar almanac", deliberately unlike last week's dark wizard sky.
  - Silver-blue paper background with a soft moon glowing in the top-right corner.
  - Cormorant Garamond for headings and Karla for body text.
  - A row of all eight phase emoji at the top of the intro and result cards.
  - Each answer has a small moon outline that half-fills on hover and turns full and white when picked.
  - Result cards get a top border and trait pills in that outcome's colour.

**Left as is:**

- "A single, perfect candle" → Last Quarter (minimalism) is a softer link than most.
