## Outcomes

### 💚 Jay Gatsby
*A dreamer with a plan, a mansion and a light across the bay.*

You believe in big hopes and grand gestures, and you're willing to reinvent yourself to reach them. Your optimism is magnetic: people turn up to whatever you've organised, even if they're not sure who you really are. Just remember the future is allowed to be different from the past.

Traits: hopeful, ambitious, generous

### 🌼 Daisy Buchanan
*Charming, breezy and impossible to forget.*

You light up a room without trying, and people lean in when you talk. You love beautiful things, lazy afternoons and anything that keeps the mood light. Underneath the sparkle you feel things deeply, even if you'd rather not make a fuss about it.

Traits: charming, graceful, spontaneous

### 📓 Nick Carraway
*The thoughtful observer everyone ends up confiding in.*

You'd rather watch the party than be its centre, and you notice everything. People trust you with their secrets because you listen and don't rush to judge. You're loyal, quietly funny and the one who remembers how the whole story really went.

Traits: observant, loyal, reflective

### ⛳ Jordan Baker
*Cool, confident and always a step ahead.*

You move through life with easy self-assurance and a dry sense of humour. You're independent, sporty and happiest when you're doing things your own way. Nobody rattles you, and you'd never wait around for someone else to make the plan.

Traits: confident, independent, witty

### 🦉 Owl Eyes
*The curious guest who wandered into the library and found the real story.*

While everyone else is dancing, you're checking whether the books on the shelves are real. You're delightfully curious, easily amazed and far more perceptive than people expect. You're the one who sees through the show and still shows up when it counts.

Traits: curious, perceptive, sincere

### 🏇 Tom Buchanan
*Bold, competitive and never one to sit on the sidelines.*

You throw yourself into everything at full strength, from a weekend match to a long day outdoors. You love a good contest and you love winning, and you're never shy about saying what you think. People always know exactly where they stand with you, and you bring serious energy to any team you're on.

Traits: bold, sporty, decisive

### 💃 Myrtle Wilson
*Full of life and hungry for something bigger.*

You have more energy than any room can hold, and you're not afraid to want more from life. You can turn an ordinary afternoon into an impromptu party, and you light up when things get glamorous. Your zest is contagious, and people remember an evening with you long after it ends.

Traits: vivacious, daring, sociable

### 🔧 George Wilson
*Steady hands, a quiet heart and a job done properly.*

You're the dependable one who keeps things running while everyone else is busy making a scene. You're patient, hardworking and devoted to the people you love, even if you don't say much about it. Nobody notices how much you hold together until you take a day off.

Traits: dependable, devoted, hardworking

## Agent notes

## Design choices

- **8 outcomes, winner scoring.** Gatsby, Daisy, Nick and Jordan are the best-known characters. The owner asked to add Tom, Myrtle and George Wilson, and Owl Eyes (the guest amazed that the library books are real) is the rarest result (~7%). The characters are distinct types, so "winner" fits better than "blend". Wolfsheim is still left out because he's written as an ethnic caricature.
- **Writing the darker characters.** Tom, Myrtle and George are written warmly as personality types, with none of the book's violence, racism, affair or deaths:
  - Tom: sporty and competitive
  - Myrtle: vivacious and wanting more from life
  - George: dependable and devoted
- **11 questions.** With 8 outcomes and at most 6 answers per question, every question covers 5–6 characters, and a few answers share points. The mix is scenarios (party arrival, a secret, running late, moving house), either/or picks ("One great love / adventure / fresh start…"), preferences (afternoon, skill, home, compliment, night sound) and a motto.
- **Theme:** an Art Deco party night. Black-lacquer teal background with faint gold fan scallops, gold double-ruled card frames with a sunburst fan at the top, Poiret One display type and uppercase Josefin Sans. A picked answer fills with gold. Result cards take their outline from the outcome colour. The theme respects reduced motion.

## Critic notes

- **Rounds 1–2 (5 outcomes):** fixed a bolted-on museum answer, an odd "how they found out" answer and the giveaway "Pick a colour" question (Green → Gatsby). Also fixed overlapping "text ahead" answers, "Your voice" (not parallel with the other answers) and honesty being tested three times.
- **Round 3 (after adding Tom, Myrtle and George):**
  - The critic confirmed the three new outcomes are warm and recognisable and don't reference the dark plot.
  - Blocking: Q11 "Pick a weekend treat" repeated Q2, and "catching up on jobs" isn't a treat. I replaced it with "Pick a motto".
  - Also taken from the non-blocking notes:
    - Tom's description loses "heated debate", "tradition" and a preachy closing line.
    - "Insist everyone else was early" (unflattering for Tom) is replaced.
    - "Make a grand entrance anyway" now goes to Tom and Myrtle, because Gatsby famously stays hidden at his own parties. Gatsby gets "Promise to make it up to them in style".
    - "Public speaking" became "Acting" for Gatsby, which fits his self-invention.
    - "Horse riding" no longer gives Jordan a point.
- **Left as is:** "One great love" and "Keep an eye on the door for one particular guest" are clear Gatsby nods, which I kept as fun fan service. Q6 has two pairs of houses that feel similar (old houses, grand estates), but each pair is different enough (old-money townhouse vs. odd rambling house, new mansion vs. country estate).
