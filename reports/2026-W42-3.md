## Outcomes

### 🎯 The Straight Shooter
*Say it plainly, sort it quickly, move on.*

You'd rather have an awkward five minutes than a tense five weeks. You name the problem, say where you stand and expect others to do the same. People always know where they are with you, and that honesty is a gift, even when it arrives a little fast.

Traits: honest, decisive, brave

### 🌉 The Bridge Builder
*Not your side or mine: let's find a better one.*

You treat a disagreement like a puzzle that both people are solving together. You ask questions, you listen for what someone actually needs, and you keep going until everyone walks away feeling heard. It takes longer, and the results tend to last.

Traits: curious, patient, fair

### 🕊️ The Peacekeeper
*Keeping the room warm is your quiet superpower.*

You notice when someone is upset before they've said a word, and you'll happily give a little ground to keep things kind. Friends feel safe around you. Just remember your own wishes count too: the people who love you want to hear them.

Traits: kind, generous, perceptive

### 🤝 The Dealmaker
*Half each, shake on it, let's get dinner.*

You're practical about disagreements: everyone gives a bit, everyone gets a bit, and nobody spends the whole evening arguing. You're brilliant at spotting the reasonable middle and talking people towards it. Group trips run smoother with you in charge of the plan.

Traits: practical, reasonable, quick-thinking

### 🧊 The Cool Head
*Step back, breathe, then come back wiser.*

You know that not every argument needs to happen right now. You take a walk, let the heat drain out, and return when you can say what you actually mean. Plenty of fights simply dissolve on their own while you're away, and the ones that don't go far better.

Traits: calm, reflective, steady

### 🃏 The Tension Breaker
*One well-timed joke and everyone exhales.*

You have a knack for finding the funny side just when the room needs it most. A light word from you lowers shoulders and reminds everyone they actually like each other. Once people are laughing again, the real conversation tends to get a lot easier.

Traits: witty, warm, disarming

## Agent notes

### Design choices
- **9 questions, 6 outcomes, blend scoring.** Conflict styles overlap: most people mix two or three, so blend shows the mix. The outcomes are Straight Shooter (direct), Bridge Builder (collaborative), Peacekeeper (accommodating), Dealmaker (compromise), Cool Head (step back first) and Tension Breaker (humour). That's the classic five conflict modes reframed positively, plus a playful sixth.
- The questions mix everyday scenarios (dishes, choosing a restaurant, pizza, a friend cancelling, feedback), preferences (superpower, game) and a "which line sounds like you" pick.
- **Theme: "talking it out".** Answers are chat bubbles that lean alternately left and right (two sides of a conversation). The question is the incoming message, there's a two-tone pastel backdrop, and the result card is a speech bubble with a tail in the outcome's colour. Fonts are Young Serif and Figtree.

### Critic notes
The critic ran four times. It flagged and I fixed:
- Q1: "Wait a week to see if it's really a pattern" contradicted "keeps".
- Q2: the group-chat answers weren't parallel ("Nothing yet"), and the question overlapped with Q6. It is now a restaurant-choice scenario.
- Q3: "Perfect comic timing" was a skill, not a superpower. "Lie detection" overlapped with mind reading. "Super speed" and "X-ray vision" had weak links. The answer is now "Fearlessness", and persuasion goes mainly to the Dealmaker.
- Q4: added a "You…" prompt and "you and a friend".
- Q5: removed two-idea feelings ("Annoyed, and you'll tell them so"). The answers are now single words.
- Q6: replaced the two-sentence "Truce? I'll buy snacks." and the snack line, which overlapped with "Can I get back to you?".
- Q7: the colleague question overlapped with Q2. It is now about receiving feedback.
- Q8: mixed genres and specific games. It now uses named games. I swapped out "Pandemic" because of the no-health rule.
- Renamed the outcome id `jester` to `tension-breaker`.

What I left, with reasons:
- "Instant persuasion" scoring mostly Dealmaker is a looser link.
- "Fed up" in Q5 is the natural reaction, so it pulls a little toward Straight Shooter.
- "Laugh it off" (Q7) is a bit dismissive for the Tension Breaker.
All judged acceptable.

### Uncertainties

- The Dealmaker is the rarest result (about 11%).
- Check that the alternating bubble offsets (8% margin) look fine at 390 px.
