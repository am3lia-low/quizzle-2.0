## Outcomes

### 🍦 Vanilla Bean
*The human equivalent of a warm kitchen on a cold night.*

You make people feel at home within five minutes of meeting them. There's always a spare blanket, a second helping and a good excuse to stay a bit longer. Comfort is a superpower, and you've got it in spades.

Traits: warm, generous, homey

### 🍋 Citrus Grove
*Bright, zesty and impossible to be grumpy around.*

You walk into a room and the energy goes up a notch. You're the first to suggest the spontaneous plan and the last to run out of steam. Mornings don't scare you, and somehow you make everyone else a bit more awake too.

Traits: energetic, upbeat, spontaneous

### 🧺 Fresh Linen
*Calm, clean and quietly running the whole show.*

You like things just so, and honestly, life goes better when you're in charge of it. Your calm is contagious, your lists are legendary and your space always feels like a deep breath. People trust you because you follow through.

Traits: organised, calm, dependable

### 🌲 Cedar & Smoke
*Grounded, a little mysterious, and best by a fire.*

You're the deep end of the friend group: thoughtful, steady and happiest somewhere with trees and no signal. You don't say much in a crowd, but one-on-one you're the person everyone wants to talk to until 2am.

Traits: grounded, thoughtful, outdoorsy

### 🌊 Sea Salt & Driftwood
*Breezy, unbothered and always half on holiday.*

You go with the tide and it usually takes you somewhere good. Plans are suggestions, sand gets everywhere and that's fine. Your easygoing attitude is a gift to anyone who's ever stressed about the little things.

Traits: free-spirited, relaxed, adventurous

### 🌹 Rose & Peony
*A soft heart with excellent taste.*

You notice the details everyone else misses: the handwritten note, the perfect flower, the song that fits the moment. You love big and you love well, and the people in your life never doubt how much they matter to you.

Traits: romantic, devoted, expressive

## Agent notes

## Design choices
- **9 questions, 6 outcomes:** candle scents have clear "moods" (cosy, zesty, clean, woodsy, coastal, romantic), and six covers the classic scent families without overlap. Nine questions gives each outcome enough chances to show up while keeping it quick.
- **Blend scoring:** scents are an aesthetic and people are rarely one note, so showing the mix (e.g. mostly Cedar & Smoke with a bit of Vanilla Bean) fits the topic.
- **Theme:** a dim room lit by a single candle. Dark brown background with a warm flame-coloured glow rising from the bottom, a gently flickering emoji (off under reduced motion), wax-like answer buttons that glow on hover, and each result card glowing in its outcome's colour. Cormorant Garamond for headings, Lato for body.

## Critic notes
- **Round 1 (blocking):** Q9 "Voice notes from wherever you are" didn't answer "describe your texts" and steered to Sea Salt. Q9 answers were also mixed in shape. Q8 "Cicadas on a summer night" was likely factually off (most cicadas sing by day) and steered with a season. **Changed:** Q9 rewritten as parallel "X and Y" descriptions. Q8 answer replaced with "Distant city traffic". Also trimmed the borderline steers "in the sunshine" and "with no plan" from Q1, and removed the slightly backhanded "You're not boring" from Vanilla Bean.
- **Round 2 (blocking):** Q9 had overlapping pairs ("Long and heartfelt" / "Warm and chatty", "Short and breezy" / "Neat and to the point"), and "breezy" echoed the Sea Salt tagline. **Changed:** dropped "Warm and chatty" (Q9 now has 5 answers) and replaced "Short and breezy" with "Carefree and typo-filled". Also changed Q1 "Hike until your phone loses signal" to "Go for a long hike", because it echoed the Cedar description. Changed Rose & Peony's trait "thoughtful" to "devoted" so it no longer duplicates Cedar's.
- **Left as is (non-blocking):** "Seersucker" is a slightly obscure fabric, but it's a fun word and an apt summery pick. "A crackling fire" → Cedar and "Waves on the shore" → Sea Salt are literal but natural. The links for Q6 "Mid-afternoon" (vanilla/sea-salt) and the Q7 middle answers are loose. These are small scoring nudges.

## Uncertainties
- Q3 "Seersucker": check that players will know it.
- Q8 "Distant city traffic" → Citrus Grove is a weak link. Consider whether a different energetic sound would read better.
- Vanilla Bean is now the rarest result (~13.5%) after Q9 lost its vanilla answer. That still looks fine.
