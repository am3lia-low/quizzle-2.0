## Outcomes

### 🚀 Monday
*Fresh start, clean slate, and a to-do list already in motion.*

Everyone else groans when you arrive, but secretly they need you. You're the fresh start: new notebook, new plan, new energy. You love a goal, you love a clean slate, and you have a rare gift for getting things moving while others are still finding their shoes.

Traits: driven, organised, optimistic

### 🧱 Tuesday
*Steady, dependable and quietly getting everything done.*

You're the day that actually does the work. No fanfare, no drama, just a reliable rhythm that keeps the whole week standing up. People trust you because you show up, you follow through, and you never make a fuss about it. Underrated? Maybe. Essential? Absolutely.

Traits: reliable, practical, low-key

### ⚖️ Wednesday
*The balanced middle that holds everything together.*

You're the bridge between where things started and where they're going. You see both sides, you keep the peace, and you're the friend everyone calls when they need someone level-headed to talk it through. You make the hump feel like a gentle hill.

Traits: balanced, diplomatic, easy to talk to

### ✨ Thursday
*Almost there, and making the anticipation half the fun.*

You live for the build-up. You're the one who books the table, starts the countdown and gets everyone excited about what's coming next. Your optimism is contagious, and somehow the plans you hype up always turn out even better than promised.

Traits: hopeful, enthusiastic, forward-looking

### 🎉 Friday
*The week's favourite. Doors open, music up, let's go.*

You're the one everybody's been waiting for. You bring the energy, the playlist and the excuse to celebrate, and people instantly relax when you walk in. You know how to switch off from the serious stuff and remind everyone that life is meant to be enjoyed.

Traits: fun, sociable, spontaneous

### 🧭 Saturday
*Free, curious and already halfway out the door.*

You're wide open possibility. No alarm, no agenda, just a full day and a feeling that anything could happen. You chase new places and new experiences, you say yes to the unusual, and you come back from every adventure with a story worth hearing.

Traits: adventurous, free-spirited, curious

### ☕ Sunday
*Slow mornings, long lunches and a deeply calming presence.*

You're the deep breath at the end of it all. You know the value of slowing down, you make every space feel cosier, and you're brilliant at savouring the moment instead of rushing past it. Time with you feels like a lie-in with nowhere to be.

Traits: calm, cosy, reflective

## Agent notes

## Design choices
- **7 outcomes** (preset by the topic): one per day, each with a positive personality — Monday the fresh start, Tuesday the steady doer, Wednesday the balanced middle, Thursday the anticipation, Friday the fun, Saturday the adventurer, Sunday the calm.
- **10 questions:** a mix of picks (time of day, shoes, smell), scenarios (free afternoon, friend's big decision, trip planning) and two either/or questions. That's enough to separate seven outcomes.
- **Winner scoring:** each day is its own distinct type rather than a style you'd mix, so a single result reads better than a blend.
- **Theme:** a desk planner. The page is cream ruled paper with a red margin line. Cards look like tear-off calendar pages: a red header strip with two binding-ring holes, and on the result card the strip takes the outcome's colour. The fonts are DM Serif Display and DM Sans. The margin line is dropped on narrow phones.
